## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ Schema)
### Q: Is my JSON data kept private?
**A:** Yes. All JSONPath parsing and evaluation runs 100% locally in your browser with zero data transmitted to any external server.

### Q: What JSONPath operators are supported?
**A:** Supports root ($), property access (.), wildcards (*), recursive descent (..), slice indexes ([0:2]), and filter expressions.
