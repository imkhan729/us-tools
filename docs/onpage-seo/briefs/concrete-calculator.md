## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ Schema)
### Q: How many 80lb bags of concrete in a cubic yard?
**A:** It takes 45 bags of 80lb concrete (or 60 bags of 60lb concrete) to make 1 cubic yard.

### Q: What is the formula for slab concrete?
**A:** Length (ft) x Width (ft) x Thickness (inches) / 324 = Cubic Yards.
