**A:** Based on a standard 40-hour work week (2,080 hours per year), $50,000 is approximately $24.04 per hour.

### Q: How many working hours in a standard year?
**A:** A standard full-time year (52 weeks at 40 hours per week) equals 2,080 working hours.
