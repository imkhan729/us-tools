- **Title Tag Integrity:** 100% unique title tags across the inventory, aligned with brand identifier.

- **Schema Markup Coverage:** 100% of pages include JSON-LD graphs (`WebApplication`, `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage`, and `HowTo`).
